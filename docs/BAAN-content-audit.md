# BAAN B content integration audit

## Sources reviewed

- [BAAN B homepage](https://baanb.in/)
- [BAAN B contact page](https://baanb.in/contact-us/)

The accessible pages identify BAAN B as a salon, beauty, spa and bridal destination in Madhapur, Hyderabad. The integrated business details are the published contact email (`info@baanb.in`), published address, daily hours of 09:00 AM–10:00 PM, and WhatsApp booking link.

The homepage header publishes `+91 9550728155`, while the contact page publishes `+91 7993425252`. Both are retained in the shared configuration until the owner confirms the preferred primary number.

## Service catalogue status

The live site exposes the service categories and links for salon, spa and bridal services, but the individual detail URLs were inaccessible during this audit. Exact BAAN B service prices, durations, descriptions and source images therefore remain owner-review items. Existing local catalogue entries were preserved to avoid breaking the current booking flow and are not described as live-site-verified.

The homepage also presents the claims `15+ Years of Experience`, `87000+ Client Served`, `100% Quality Service` and `86800+ Happy Clients`; these are stored as source claims and flagged for owner verification before being treated as audited business facts.
